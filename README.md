## 🎯 Direct Alignment with Problem Statement
> **Problem Statement:** *"Interior designers frequently exchange design concepts with clients and need to record which layouts, materials, and color schemes have been approved or require changes."*

Our system provides a specialized **3-Pillar Review Engine**:
1. **Layout Review & Approval:** Record sign-off on furniture layouts and room flow.
2. **Material Selection & Finishes:** Technical swatches (veneer, quartz, fabric, tiles, metal trims) with codes and statuses.
3. **Color Scheme Review:** Wall paint codes (e.g. Asian Paints) and accent schemes.

---

## 🚀 Key Features Built
- **Project Spaces & Room Navigator:** Living Room, Master Bedroom, Modular Kitchen (with dynamic space addition).
- **Concept A/B Options:** Compare different architectural directions per space with 3D render angle galleries.
- **Granular 3-Pillar Status:** Instant click-to-approve or click-to-revise for Layout, Material, and Color Scheme individually.
- **Role Toggle (Client vs Lead Designer):** Switch between the homeowner and lead architect perspectives.
- **Interactive Feedback & Audit Trail:** Real-time design notes and auto-generated approval logs.
- **Client Sign-off Summary Sheet:** Modal summarizing all room approvals, ready to print or save as PDF.
- **Offline / LocalStorage Persistence:** Auto-saves selections and includes a 1-click **Reset Demo** button.
